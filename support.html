Common questions

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

On your old iPhone, open Modicum, go to Plan, and tap Back up. Save the file somewhere your new iPhone can reach, such as iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, install Modicum and tap Restore from a backup on the welcome screen, or Plan and then Restore if you've already started.

How often should I back up?

Everything lives only on your iPhone, so a backup every week or so, and before switching phones, keeps it safe. Modicum reminds you on the Today screen when a backup is due.

How are my targets worked out?

Modicum starts from a standard estimate of how many calories you burn, based on your age, height, weight, and activity. As you log food and weigh in, it compares what you eat with how your weight trend actually moves, and adjusts your targets toward what you really burn. Plan shows the details under How the goal is calculated. Modicum never sets a goal below 1,200 calories for women or 1,500 for men.

A food isn't in the list. What can I do?

Search for it in Add food, then tap Create as a food at the end of the results. Enter the numbers from the label or the restaurant's website, and it's saved for next time. Tap the star to keep it in Favorites.

Why isn't my favorite restaurant included?

Modicum only includes restaurants that publish their own nutrition guide. You can still add any restaurant item yourself, as above.

I got a new iPhone. Do I have to pay again?

No. In Modicum, restore your purchase with the same Apple Account you bought it with. That restores the purchase only; restore a backup file to bring your logs.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Plan and tap Erase everything. It asks first, then deletes all your data from your iPhone and starts fresh. Deleting the app does the same.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles purchases and refunds. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
