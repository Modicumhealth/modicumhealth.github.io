Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Everything you enter in Modicum, including your profile, food logs, and weigh-ins, stays on your iPhone. The app never sends it to us or to anyone else. We collect no data about you.

What Modicum stores, and where

Modicum saves what you enter on your iPhone so the app can work:

  • Your profile: sex, age, height, weight, activity level, and goals
  • Food logs, weigh-ins, your daily goals, and your own foods, recipes, meals, and favorites
  • Small settings, such as light or dark appearance and when you last made a backup

This stays in Modicum's own storage on your iPhone. Modicum does not sync it to iCloud or send it anywhere. We run no servers that receive it and have no way to see it.

If you back up your whole iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, that backup can include Modicum's data, as it does for most apps. Those backups are between you and Apple, or stay on your computer. We never receive them.

What Modicum does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking
  • No third-party code or SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of what you enter, which we never receive

Modicum doesn't need the internet to work. Its food list is built into the app, so searching and logging happen entirely on your iPhone.

The App Store, the free trial, and your purchase

Apple handles downloading Modicum, the free trial, the purchase, and restoring a purchase, under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your name, Apple Account details, or payment details from Apple.

Apple may give us summary statistics, such as download counts, and crash reports from people who chose to share them with app developers in their iPhone's settings. These don't include anything you entered in Modicum.

Backups

When you make a backup, Modicum creates a file and lets you choose where to save it, such as Files, iCloud Drive, or a USB drive. From then on, that file is yours, and wherever you keep it handles it under its own policy. The file isn't encrypted, so anyone who has it can read what's in it, including your weight and what you eat; keep it somewhere private. Modicum only reads a backup file when you choose to restore it.

Health information

Your weight, body measurements, and food logs are sensitive. They stay on your iPhone and are never shared with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else.

Modicum does not currently connect to Apple Health. If it ever does, we will update this policy first. Health data would never be used for advertising and never stored in iCloud by Modicum.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, or scripts. Like any website, it is delivered by a hosting provider, which receives the technical details that come with every web request, such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested, and may keep them in its server logs. Nothing you enter in the app is ever part of this.

Children

Modicum is for adults 18 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and what anyone enters in the app stays on their iPhone.

Deleting your data

In Modicum, go to Plan and tap Erase everything to delete all of it from your iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes it. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete. We never receive what you enter, so we hold no copy of it to delete.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR in the EU and UK or state privacy laws in the US give you rights to access, correct, or delete personal data a company holds about you. We don't hold what you enter in Modicum; you can see, change, or delete all of it yourself in the app. For information Apple holds about your purchase, contact Apple. You're always welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here and update the effective date. If Modicum ever starts sending any of what you enter off your iPhone, we will update this policy before that happens and ask for your permission in the app.
