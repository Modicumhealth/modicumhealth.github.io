Accessibility

Effective October 2, 2026

Modicum should work well for everyone, including people who use VoiceOver, larger text, or other accessibility settings on iPhone.

What Modicum does

  • Buttons, charts, and numbers have spoken labels for VoiceOver. The weight chart is read as one description of your trend, and the calorie budget is read in words ("1,416 calories left").
  • Confirmations and warnings, such as "Added" or a message about an amount, are spoken as well as shown.
  • Undo after removing something stays available while VoiceOver is on it.
  • Actions you can swipe for, such as deleting a weigh-in, are also in VoiceOver's Actions menu, so no swipe is needed.
  • Modicum uses the iPhone's own controls and text styles, so it follows your text size and bold text settings in most places.
  • It works in light and dark appearance, and you can choose either one on the Plan screen.

Known limitations

Modicum has not yet had an independent accessibility audit or a full test with VoiceOver on every screen, and its color contrast hasn't been formally measured. At the largest text sizes, some numbers on the Today screen shrink to fit rather than wrapping. If something is hard to use, please tell us.

Feedback

If you run into a barrier, email support.modicum@proton.me with what you were trying to do and which accessibility settings you use. We aim to reply within a few days.
